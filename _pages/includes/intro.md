I'm a PhD student at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, working under the guidance of Professor [Huamin Qu (屈华民)](http://www.huamin.org/) and Associate Professor [Xiaojun Ma (麻晓娟)](https://www.cse.ust.hk/~mxj/). I hold a Master of Fine Art in Information Art and Design from Tsinghua University (China), a Master of Science in Communication Design from the Polytechnic University of Milan (Italy), and a Bachelor of Fine Art in Film and Television Photography and Production from the Central Academy of Fine Arts (China). My current research interests lie in AI storytelling and AI integration with design.
