I'm a PhD candidate at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, working under the guidance of Professor [Huamin Qu (屈华民)](http://www.huamin.org/) and Associate Professor [Xiaojun Ma (麻晓娟)](https://www.cse.ust.hk/~mxj/). My research focuses on Human-Computer Interaction and Human-AI Interaction for creativity. I hold a Master of Fine Art in Information Art and Design from Tsinghua University (China), a Master of Science in Communication Design from the Polytechnic University of Milan (Italy), and a Bachelor of Fine Art in Film and Television Photography and Production from the Central Academy of Fine Arts (China). Before joining HKUST, I worked with Professor [Antti Oulasvirta](https://users.aalto.fi/~oulasvir/) at Aalto University (Finland). My current research interests lie in AI storytelling and AI integration with design.
